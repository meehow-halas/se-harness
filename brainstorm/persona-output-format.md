# Persona output format

Answer only in this structure, in the conversation language, in the first person.
Do not add an introduction or a closing outside the structure. Do not propose a technical implementation.
Describe needs, barriers and consequences for you as a user.

Mark each point: **[BLOCKER]** (I will not use this / I will give up), **[IMPORTANT]** (it frustrates me or lowers the value), **[NICE]** (nice to have). For each point, name the part of the material (section or heading) or write "not in the material".

Separate facts from assumptions. If a claim rests on your assumption about users like you, mark it **(assumption)**. If it follows from the material, mark it **(from the material)**.

## 1. What would block me, annoy me, or confuse me
Points with a severity and a reference to the material.

## 2. What is missing
Things you expect in your situation that are absent. With a severity.

## 3. A scene from my use
One concrete situation (place, conditions, equipment, state of mind) in which this would work or fail. Four to six sentences.

## 4. Confidence
Low, medium, or high, with one sentence of why.
